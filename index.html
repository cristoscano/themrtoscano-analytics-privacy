Privacy Policy – TheMrToscano Analytics

Last updated: November 6, 2026

TheMrToscano Analytics is a private, internal tool operated by Toscano Media for the owner of the TheMrToscano YouTube channel. It is not offered to the public.

Data we access

With the channel owner's authorization, the app uses read-only Google scopes (youtube.readonly and yt-analytics.readonly) to read the channel's own video list and YouTube Analytics metrics such as views, average view percentage, watch time and audience retention.

How we use it

Data is used solely to produce internal performance reports for the channel owner. We do not sell, share, or transfer this data to third parties, and we do not use it for advertising.

Storage and security

Reports and access tokens are stored privately and securely and are accessible only to the channel owner and his authorized tools. No data from other YouTube users is collected.

Revoking access

You can revoke access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. Use of YouTube data is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service.

Contact

Questions: open an issue at this site's repository or contact the channel owner via YouTube @themrtoscano.
